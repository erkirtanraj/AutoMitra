# AutoMitra Business Plan

## 1. Business model

AutoMitra is an asset-light automotive marketplace. It does not initially purchase or hold vehicle inventory. It connects buyers, individual sellers, dealers and properly authorized service partners.

## 2. Target-state monthly revenue model

| Revenue stream | Monthly target |
|---|---:|
| Dealer subscriptions | ₹1,50,000 |
| Featured listings | ₹50,000 |
| Buyer lead generation | ₹1,25,000 |
| Display advertising | ₹75,000 |
| Finance/insurance partner referrals | ₹75,000 |

| **Total** | **₹5,00,000** |

These are illustrative targets, not guaranteed or validated market rates. Partner income depends on written agreements, eligibility, regulatory permissions and completed qualifying actions.

## 4. Inventory target

- 85 dealers × 15 active vehicles = 1,275 dealer listings.
- Target 725 individual seller listings.
- Target total: approximately 2,000 active used-car listings.
- Maintain a separate structured new-car catalogue; catalogue models are not equivalent to available stock.

Listings must be moderated, freshness-checked and marked sold/expired promptly.

## 5. Lead revenue assumptions

| Lead type | Monthly quantity | Assumed price | Revenue |

| New-car enquiries | 200 | ₹250 | ₹50,000 |

Bill only valid, consented and contactable enquiries under transparent dealer terms. Define duplicate, invalid, unreachable and refund rules. Do not share leads beyond the scope disclosed to the customer.

## 7. Advertising assumptions

Target display advertising revenue: ₹75,000/month.

Illustrative pageview requirement:
- At ₹50 page RPM: 1,500,000 pageviews.
- At ₹100 page RPM: 750,000 pageviews.
- At ₹150 page RPM: 500,000 pageviews.
- At ₹200 page RPM: 375,000 pageviews.

RPM is an assumption, not a promise. Actual earnings depend on geography, ad fill, viewability, content quality, consent and advertiser demand. Ad approval is not guaranteed.

## 8. Partner referral assumptions

Illustrative target: ₹75,000/month from finance and insurance-related partner activity. Do not launch referral monetization until legal review and suitable authorized partner agreements are complete. Do not represent AutoMitra as a lender, insurer, broker or regulated intermediary unless it is duly authorized.

## 9. Sponsored promotion assumptions

Illustrative target: ₹25,000/month from dealer banners, sponsored reviews and brand campaigns. Clearly disclose paid placements and maintain editorial separation.

## 10. Traffic target

A planning target of 200,000 monthly visits could be allocated as:
- Organic search: 100,000.
- Direct: 20,000.
- Social: 25,000.
- Dealer referrals: 15,000.
- Paid campaigns: 30,000.
- Other referrals: 10,000.

This is an acquisition target, not current traffic or a forecast. Build useful original model, comparison, city and editorial pages. Avoid thin or duplicated SEO pages.

## 14. Risk controls

- Dealer churn: demonstrate enquiry quality and inventory analytics.
- Fake listings: moderation, verification and reporting.
- Low lead quality: consent, phone verification, deduplication and feedback.
- Ad dependency: diversify revenue.
- Partner compliance: written agreements and legal review.
- High CAC: track payback by channel.
- Pricing inaccuracies: source and timestamp all prices.
- Cash runway: hire gradually and maintain a rolling 13-week cash forecast.
