# AutoMitra Delivery Roadmap

## Phase 0 — foundation
- Confirm brand, PRD and UI tokens.
- Create repository structure and development conventions.
- Configure environments, CI and secrets handling.
- Approve database and API contracts.

## Phase 1 — catalogue and discovery
- Brands, models and variants.
- New-car catalogue and specifications.
- City and price source metadata.
- Search, filters, sorting and pagination.
- SEO-friendly model and variant pages.

Acceptance: visitors can search and inspect catalogue data on mobile and desktop.

## Phase 2 — used-car marketplace
- Seller registration.
- Listing form and media upload.
- Listing moderation.
- Used-car detail and search.
- Report listing and sold/expired states.

Acceptance: seller submissions cannot become public until required moderation is complete.

## Phase 3 — dealers and enquiries
- Dealer registration and verification.
- Dealer inventory and staff permissions.
- Buyer enquiry and consent capture.
- Lead assignment and status tracking.
- Dealer response notifications.

Acceptance: dealer can only access authorized inventory and leads; all data sharing is auditable.

## Phase 4 — monetization
- Dealer plans and entitlements.
- Gateway checkout and verified webhooks.
- Featured listing promotions.
- Lead billing, disputes and reversals.
- Invoices and financial reports.

Acceptance: payment and billing flows pass replay, duplicate and refund tests.

## Phase 5 — Android
- Flutter authentication and account.
- Catalogue and search.
- Listing detail and enquiry.
- Favorites and notifications.
- Seller/dealer workflows as product scope allows.

Acceptance: Android app passes release smoke tests and API authorization tests.

## Phase 6 — growth and partners
- SEO content and analytics.
- Advertising integration.
- Sponsored campaigns.
- Authorized finance/insurance partner journeys.
- Advanced dealer CRM and reporting.

Acceptance: partner compliance review, consent review and unit economics approval complete.

## Release gates

- No critical/high unresolved security findings.
- Privacy notice and terms published.
- Payment webhook verification tested.
- Backups and restore process tested.
- Moderation and complaint process staffed.
- Monitoring and incident contacts configured.
- Pricing and listing data sources disclosed.
