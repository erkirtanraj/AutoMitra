# AutoMitra UI Guidelines

## 1. Design principles

- Trust first: clear seller identity, listing freshness and verification labels.
- Search first: prominent search and useful filters.
- Mobile first: one-column flows on phones; progressive multi-column layouts on desktop.
- Price clarity: distinguish ex-showroom, on-road, indicative and seller-quoted prices.
- Original identity: create AutoMitra's own visual language; do not reproduce competitor logos, exact layouts, proprietary imagery or trade dress.
- Accessible: readable type, visible focus, semantic controls and sufficient contrast.

## 2. Brand personality

Helpful, confident, transparent, modern and practical. Avoid exaggerated claims such as guaranteed best price or verified condition unless evidence supports them.

## 3. Layout

- Desktop: centered content container, maximum width around 1200–1280px.
- Tablet: fluid container with 24px side gutters.
- Mobile: 16px side gutters and single-column content.
- Use a consistent 8px spacing rhythm.
- Header: brand, primary navigation, location selector, account action.
- Footer: about, contact, help, legal, privacy and partner disclosures.

## 4. Typography

- Recommended family: Inter or a system sans-serif fallback.
- Page title: 28–36px desktop, 24–28px mobile.
- Section heading: 20–24px.
- Card title: 16–18px.
- Body: 14–16px.
- Metadata: 12–13px; never use tiny text for legal or price disclosures.
- Use tabular numerals for prices and metrics.

## 5. Core components

- Search bar with accessible label and clear action.
- Filter drawer on mobile; sidebar/filter toolbar on desktop.
- Vehicle card with image, make/model/variant, price type, year, fuel, transmission, location and freshness.
- Dealer badge with explicit verification meaning.
- Enquiry form with consent disclosure and privacy link.
- Comparison table with aligned specification rows.
- Listing status badge.
- Empty, loading, error and retry states.
- Pagination or accessible load-more pattern.
- Toasts for transient feedback; inline errors for form validation.

## 6. Vehicle cards

Show:
- Licensed/authorized vehicle image.
- Vehicle title and variant.
- Price and price type.
- Year, kilometres, fuel and transmission for used cars.
- City and listing age.
- Seller/dealer identity and verification status.
- Save action and enquiry CTA.

Do not show unverified claims such as accident-free, single-owner or inspected as facts without evidence.

## 7. Forms

- Ask only for necessary data.
- Group fields into clear steps for long seller forms.
- Explain why phone/email is needed.
- Show consent purpose and recipient before submission.
- Preserve user input after validation errors.
- Provide accessible labels, error text and success confirmation.

## 8. Responsive behavior

- Mobile: sticky bottom action only when it does not obscure content; filters in a drawer; compare tray with dismiss control.
- Desktop: persistent search/filter context where useful.
- Images use responsive sizes, fixed aspect ratios and lazy loading.
- Tables become labelled stacked rows or horizontal scroll with clear affordance on small screens.

## 9. Accessibility

- Keyboard-operable navigation and dialogs.
- Visible focus indicators.
- Text contrast target WCAG AA.
- Alt text for meaningful vehicle imagery.
- Decorative imagery uses empty alt text.
- Do not convey status by color alone.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## 10. Content and trust

- Date and source all pricing information.
- Label sponsored/featured placements.
- Show listing-report action.
- Explain dealer verification scope.
- Provide transparent cancellation/refund terms.
